          Got your Xteink from AliExpress or another third-party reseller and USB flashing won't connect? Those
          units often ship USB-locked. No computer needed:
          download
          update.bin,
          copy it to the SD card's root folder, then hold Power + Up (top-left button on X3)
          while powering on - the OEM bootloader flashes it straight from the card. See
          USB-locked devices in the
          PicoRead docs for details.
        

      


      

        
PicoWatch

        

          Personal fork of Watchy, an open-source E-Paper smartwatch - BLE
          notifications, weather, and more. Works on both PCB V3 (ESP32-S3)
          and real Watchy V2 hardware, auto-detected from the connected chip.
          Source & docs
